A little about me

Built out of curiosity

I'm an astrophysicist. These projects are a side practice in making ideas tangible: sometimes a research workflow needs a tool, sometimes a topic deserves a visual explanation, and sometimes a small everyday problem is worth solving. Sometimes on a Sunday afternoon, I am sitting with my tea cup and come up with these ideas, which then are thrown to capable LLMs! This collection will keep growing as new ideas come along.
